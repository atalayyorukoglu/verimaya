Verimaya drafts payments and appointments from WhatsApp; the clinic approves or edits. Corrections piled up, but where they clustered stayed invisible — fixes were guesswork.

The Tools accuracy report shows untouched approval rate, the field edited most, and questions Maya could not answer. Unanswered ones point to the knowledge base. Guesswork ends; the numbers are there.
