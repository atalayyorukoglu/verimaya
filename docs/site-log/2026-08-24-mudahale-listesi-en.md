Verimaya is the clinic panel. Did a doctor’s success rate fall, did period income slip, did an incident stay open — those sat in scattered reports unless someone went looking.

The intervention list gathers worsening doctor rates, falling income, open incidents and valuable referrals on one page. Sentences are templates; figures come from the database. No model invents findings.
